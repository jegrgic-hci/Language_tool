The method

Built on how the brain acquires language.

VraiFrench exercises are grounded in language acquisition research and cognitive science — not the passive memorization patterns of traditional apps. The difference is what you do: you speak, you hear, you correct.

Production effect

Speaking encodes. Reading doesn't.

Research on the production effect consistently shows that saying something aloud creates a far stronger memory trace than reading or typing it. Every VraiFrench exercise is oral-first — you hear, you speak, and you are scored on what you produced.

Corrective feedback

Feedback must be immediate and specific.

Delayed or vague feedback does not build skill. Per-word feedback delivered the moment you finish speaking lets you connect each correction to the exact attempt — before the memory trace fades. That precision is what turns a missed sound into a learned one.

Motor learning

Pronunciation is a motor skill.

You do not think your way to a clean liaison — you drill it until it is automatic. Like any motor skill, pronunciation improves through repeated, corrected attempts rather than memorization. The hear → speak → correct loop mirrors the same acquisition cycle studied in motor learning research.

For tutors & teachers

The nuance is yours to teach.

The liaisons that feel natural, the intonation that carries meaning, the rhythm that makes French sound alive — those come from a teacher who has lived the language. VraiFrench is not here to replace that. It is here to make sure your students arrive at every session ready for it.

  • Reserve your time for what only you can do.
    Cultural nuance, register, the feeling of real spoken French — these require a human ear and a native speaker's instinct. Let the tool handle repetition so you can handle depth.
  • Know where your student has hit a ceiling.
    See which sounds they have already internalized through practice, and which ones have stalled — the ones that will not improve without direct coaching from someone who knows how they should feel to produce.
  • Make your impact visible.
    Motivation is part of learning. Showing a student an objective score trend — not just reassurance — gives them proof that the work is working, and makes your role in that progress undeniable.
