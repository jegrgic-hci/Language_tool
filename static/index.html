Hear it. Mimic it. Score it.

A phrase plays through your headphones while the mic records simultaneously. Speak along in real time — mimicking rhythm, sounds, and flow. The phrase stays hidden until you choose to reveal it.

Pronouns

Rewire how you use French pronouns. Décodage trains your ear to catch the pronoun in fast, reduced speech. Reformulation makes you say it back the natural spoken way — dislocation, on, reduced clitics — and scores it. Pick a focus and keep the reps short.
