FRANÇAIS. Beta

A French shadowing tool built for building real pronunciation — not grammar drills.

Load a passage, listen to a native speaker read it, then shadow along. The tool scores your attempt word by word and highlights what to work on. Add words to your practice list and drill them until they stick.

▲ Beta — expect imperfections. Speech recognition and analysis will make errors. The scores are a guide, not a verdict. Use them directionally to improve your pronunciation.
◆ Chrome required. Speech recognition uses the Web Speech API, which is only supported in Chrome and Edge. Other browsers will not have microphone input.

How it works
  • Active Listening opens a 30-second session. Say the word repeatedly — the tool shows what it hears and a confidence percentage, so you can adjust your pronunciation in real time.
  • Short phrases: single-syllable words like pain or mot are difficult for speech APIs to detect in isolation. When a word is short, the tool suggests a carrier phrase — say that, and it still scores on the target word only.
  • Accuracy: the tool isn't perfect — French is French — but it's consistent enough to help you build reliable pronunciation habits.
